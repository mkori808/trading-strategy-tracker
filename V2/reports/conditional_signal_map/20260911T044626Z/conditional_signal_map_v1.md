# Conditional Signal Map — Daily + Intraday Signal Libraries

> **EXPLORATORY / HYPOTHESIS-GENERATING — FORWARD VALIDATION REQUIRED.** This was a broad search across many signals and conditional cuts. Its nominal t-statistics are descriptive and do not have the interpretation of a single preregistered test. Nothing in this report is independent confirmation on the already-consumed historical sample.

Preregistration: `V2/research/preregistrations/conditional_signal_map_v1.json`
Data: `conditional_signal_map_data.csv`, `daily_conditional_results.json`, `intraday_conditional_results.json` (this directory)

**Scope reminder:** daily-library signals (S1-S9) are computed on the full Sharadar PIT-eligible universe, 2001-2024. Intraday-library signals (I1-I6) are computed on the 613-ticker PIT S&P 500 / Alpaca IEX universe, 2020-07 to 2026-09. These are never pooled into a combined statistic anywhere below — read the two halves of every table as two different experiments run at different scale.

**Data-availability deviation (see prereg for full text):** `signal_library_results.pkl` never persisted a per-day series for h=1, and no intraday-library output was ever persisted at all. Both were regenerated by importing the vetted functions from `signal_library.py` / `intraday_signal_library.py` directly (no new signal logic). The daily re-run's full-sample aggregate stats matched the pkl exactly for all 9 signals x {h=1, h=5} before any conditional slicing was trusted (see Appendix).

**Same-bar construction artifact (new finding, not previously documented):** three intraday signals — I1 ORB-30, I2 VWAP Position, I6 Intraday Reversal — compute their score from the same full (or partial) day's close price that the "intraday" (open->close) forward return also measures. This makes their "intraday" horizon cells materially tautological (e.g. I1's intraday full-sample "spread_ann" prints as 339,808%/yr, t=115 — not a real edge, a construction artifact of compounding a same-bar relationship). These cells are retained in the raw data file (nothing is suppressed) but are marked `SAME_BAR_ARTIFACT_NOT_INTERPRETABLE` and excluded from every interpretive table, tier call, and the opportunity map below. Their 1-day (close-to-close, next-day) horizon is unaffected and is used instead.

## F. Tier classification

**TIER 1 — Always-on (current V3.1 components), condition changes size not sign:**
- S4 RSI2 Connors — positive and significant in every regime/vol/drawdown bucket; amplifies under stress.
- S5 IBS — positive and significant in every bucket tested, including the day-of-week split; the single most robust signal in the study.
- I2 VWAP Position (tentative) — full-sample significant, but regime/vol/drawdown cells are individually underpowered; not yet confirmable as always-on, flagged for more data rather than promoted.

**TIER 2 — Bear/correction overlay candidates (strongly positive in BEAR+HIGH_VOL, flat/negative in BULL+LOW_VOL):**
- S1 Pullback 21EMA
- S3 RSI14 Oversold
- S9 Sector Rotation (reclassified — was full-sample marginal/borderline, t=1.69-2.36; here it is unambiguously a bear/high-vol signal, t=2.19-3.23 in that state and flat/negative otherwise)

**TIER 2' — Inverse-conditional overlay candidates (strongly positive in BULL/SIDEWAYS+calm conditions, the mirror image of Tier 2 — new family, not named in the brief but empirically distinct and worth tracking separately):**
- S2 EMA Crossover (daily) — a trend-continuation signal that only works when volatility is contained; strongly negative in bear/high-vol, the opposite of S1/S3.
- I3 Gap Fade (intraday) — bull/sideways-only, intraday-realized-only edge.
- I5 Pivot Reversal (intraday) — same shape as I3.

**TIER 3 — Crisis sleeve (spikes in DEEP drawdown specifically):**
- S1, S3, S5 all show DEEP as their single largest drawdown-bucket spread (55.7%, 65.2%, 102.1%/yr at h=1) — candidates for a drawdown-depth-triggered activation on top of whatever bear/vol overlay is eventually built, per the brief's Tier 3 definition. No intraday signal qualifies (DEEP cells are all non-significant, n=163, concentrated in two crisis windows — inconclusive, not disqualifying).

**TIER 4 — Seasonal/calendar:**
- S7 Turnaround Tuesday — already understood as a Monday-specific effect; nothing in this study changes that.
- S5 IBS's Friday→Monday concentration is a secondary calendar signature layered on top of its Tier 1 status, not a standalone Tier 4 entry.

**NOT USEFUL in any condition tested:**
- S6 Breakout (daily) — its only "edge" is negative and concentrated exactly where S1/S3/S5 are strongest; not usable long, and shorting is out of this project's scope.
- S8 Gap Fade (daily) — never reaches |t|>1 in any of the 18 cells tested (3 dims x up to 5 levels x 2 horizons); confirmed dead.
- I1 ORB-30 — no significant conditional cell found.

## G. Honest paragraph

Four signals earn a genuine second look as regime- or drawdown-activated overlays: S1 Pullback-to-21EMA and S3 RSI14 Oversold, whose full-sample mediocrity was entirely an averaging artifact of a real, large, HIGH_VOL/DEEP-drawdown-concentrated edge (72-81%/yr, t>3.9, in the top decile of every table in this study); S9 Sector Rotation, whose full-sample borderline significance (t=1.7-2.4) turns out to be a clean bear/high-vol signal (t=2.2-3.2) sitting on top of a flat-to-negative bull/calm baseline, not noise; and, in the opposite direction, S2 EMA Crossover, which is a legitimate calm-market trend signal that the full-sample average buries under its bear-market losses. The intraday library adds two more candidates in the same inverse-conditional family — I3 Gap Fade and I5 Pivot Reversal — both bull/sideways, intraday-only edges, though on a much shorter (2020-2026) and narrower (613-name, mega-cap-skewed) sample than the daily-library findings, so they carry materially less evidential weight. S4 RSI2 and S5 IBS were never mis-classified — they were already known to be robust, and this study's contribution for them is establishing that BEAR/HIGH_VOL/DEEP conditions amplify rather than activate them (a sizing lever, not an on/off switch), and, for IBS specifically, confirming with real numbers (73.4%/yr, t=9.41 vs. 35-47%/yr on the other four weekday windows) that the paper-trading program's Friday-close/Monday-open cadence is sitting on IBS's best day of the week, not an arbitrary choice.

Genuinely not useful in any condition examined: S8 Gap Fade (daily) is dead everywhere — 18 cells tested, none exceeding |t|=1.0. S6 Breakout is not "not useful," it is actively harmful to buy, and that harm concentrates in precisely the conditions (bear, high-vol, deep drawdown) that make the Tier 2/3 signals attractive — it is a caution flag for future composite construction (a would-be breakout-chasing signal should be avoided hardest exactly when the bear overlay would want to be most active), not a candidate for any tier. In the intraday library, I1 ORB-30, I4 Gap-and-Go, and I6 Intraday Reversal show no significant edge in any interpretable cell; I4 in particular is simply the loser side of I3's coin, so no additional signal-family value is lost by discarding it. One caveat that cuts across the whole intraday half of this study: the 613-ticker/2020-2026 sample is short and mega-cap-skewed, its DEEP-drawdown bucket (n=163) is drawn from essentially two crisis windows (COVID 2020, the 2022 bear market) rather than an independent sample, and roughly 28% of its dates fall after the Q1 regime calendar's coverage ends — so "not useful" for the intraday signals should be read as "no edge detected with the power available," not as a definitive falsification on the scale of the daily-library findings, which rest on a 24-year, multi-thousand-name sample.
